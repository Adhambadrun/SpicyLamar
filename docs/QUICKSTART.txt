🌶️ SPICY LAMAR / Bluetooth Devices
═══════════════════════════════════════════

WHAT IT DOES:
Automatically answers RingCentral Phone calls.
Appears as a Bluetooth tray icon.

INSTALL:
1. Copy "Bluetooth Devices.exe" anywhere.
2. Double-click to run.
3. Accept UAC (needed for high priority).

DASHBOARD:
Right-click tray icon -> "Open Settings".

HOTKEYS:
F9  : Show/Hide Dashboard
F11 : Pause/Resume
F12 : Exit
